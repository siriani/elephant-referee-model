# Comprehension standard

The document passes comprehension when a scientist from a neighbouring field, with no access to the authors, can restate:

1. **Question** — what is being asked or tested.
2. **Design** — what was studied (data, organisms, sites), what was compared or manipulated, what was measured, and how it was analysed.
3. **Main result** — the key quantitative finding, with its uncertainty if reported.
4. **Conclusion** — what the authors infer and how far they generalise.

Also report:
- terms, acronyms and symbols used before definition or never defined;
- symbols used with two meanings;
- figures or tables that cannot be read without the text, or text that contradicts a figure;
- places the reader had to guess.

A failure on any of the four items fails the gate.
