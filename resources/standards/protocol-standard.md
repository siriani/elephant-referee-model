# Protocol outline

1. **Title and registration** — working title; registry or repository where the protocol will be time-stamped.
2. **Background and rationale** — the gap, with references.
3. **Question and hypotheses** — each hypothesis with the result that would refute it.
4. **Design** — type of study; units (animal, pen, plot, image, camera…); comparison or intervention; randomisation and blinding.
5. **Population and data** — inclusion/exclusion; sources; collection procedure; annotation protocol.
6. **Outcomes** — one primary outcome; secondary outcomes; how and when measured.
7. **Sample size** — justification (power, precision, or constraint).
8. **Analysis plan** — primary analysis; handling of missing data and outliers; multiple comparisons; for models: split strategy, held-out set protection, selection rules, number of seeds, baselines, metrics.
9. **Success and stopping criteria.**
10. **Use of AI agents** — planned role of agents, approval gates, what will be logged.
11. **Data management and sharing** — storage, licence, release plan.
12. **Ethics, permits and risks.**
13. **Timeline and resources.**
