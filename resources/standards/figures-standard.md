# Figures standard

1. **Readable alone.** Axes labelled with quantity and unit; legend present when there is more than one series; panels lettered and referenced. Text legible at print size.
2. **Uncertainty shown and defined.** Error bars, bands or intervals are present for estimates, and the caption says what they are (SD, SE, 95% CI, range) and over what *n*.
3. **Honest encoding.** Bar charts start at zero; truncated or broken axes are marked; dual axes, 3D effects and area-for-length encodings are justified or removed; colour scales are perceptually ordered and not rainbow for ordered data.
4. **Accessible.** Information is not carried by red–green contrast alone; markers or line styles distinguish series.
5. **Agrees with the text.** Values, directions and group names in the figure match the text, tables and captions.
6. **No duplication.** The same figure, or the same data re-plotted, does not appear twice (for example in the main text and the appendix) without saying so.
7. **Necessary.** Each figure supports a claim the text makes; decorative or redundant figures are flagged for removal.
8. **Images of data.** Micrographs, photos and video frames carry scale bars where size matters, and any processing (cropping, contrast, splicing) is stated.

Severity: a figure that misrepresents a result is **major**; missing units, undefined error bars or unreadable text are **minor**, unless the main claim depends on that figure, in which case they are **major**.
